Health Duas

Daily Islamic supplications related to Health, with Arabic, English transliteration, meaning and source references.

DUA 31

Dua of Prophet Ayyub

أَنِّي مَسَّنِيَ الضُّرُّ وَأَنْتَ أَرْحَمُ الرَّاحِمِينَ
Transliteration
Annee massaniyad-durru wa Anta arhamur-raahimeen
Meaning
Indeed, adversity has touched me, and You are the Most Merciful of the merciful.
Reference: Surah Al-Anbiya 21:83
DUA 32

For Healing

أَذْهِبِ الْبَاسَ رَبَّ النَّاسِ، اشْفِ أَنْتَ الشَّافِي، لَا شِفَاءَ إِلَّا شِفَاؤُكَ
Transliteration
Adhhib il-ba'sa Rabban-naas, ishfi Antash-Shaafee, laa shifaa'a illaa shifaa'uk
Meaning
Remove the affliction, O Lord of mankind. Heal, for You are the Healer; there is no healing except Your healing.
Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari: 5675
DUA 66

Protection for Body

اللَّهُمَّ عَافِنِي فِي بَدَنِي، اللَّهُمَّ عَافِنِي فِي سَمْعِي، اللَّهُمَّ عَافِنِي فِي بَصَرِي
Transliteration
Allaahumma 'aafinee fee badanee, Allaahumma 'aafinee fee sam'ee, Allaahumma 'aafinee fee basaree
Meaning
O Allah, grant me health in my body; O Allah, grant me health in my hearing; O Allah, grant me health in my sight.
Reference: Sunan Abi Dawud: 5090
DUA 86

For Good Health

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْعَفْوَ وَالْعَافِيَةَ
Transliteration
Allaahumma innee as'alukal-'afwa wal-'aafiyah
Meaning
O Allah, I ask You for pardon and well-being.
Reference: Sunan Abi Dawud: 5074
DUA 104

Visiting the Sick

أَسْأَلُ اللَّهَ الْعَظِيمَ رَبَّ الْعَرْشِ الْعَظِيمِ أَنْ يَشْفِيَكَ
Transliteration
As'alullaahal-'Azeema Rabbal-'Arshil-'Azeemi an yashfiyak
Meaning
I ask Allah the Mighty, Lord of the Mighty Throne, to cure you.
Reference: Sunan Abi Dawud: 3106
