Travel Duas

Daily Islamic supplications related to Travel, with Arabic, English transliteration, meaning and source references.

DUA 15

Leaving Home

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ، تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى اللَّهِ، لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ
Transliteration
Bismillaahi, tawakkaltu 'alallaah, laa hawla wa laa quwwata illaa billaah
Meaning
In the name of Allah; I place my trust in Allah. There is no power and no strength except with Allah.
Reference: Sunan Abi Dawud: 5095
DUA 49

For a Blessed Home

رَبِّ أَنْزِلْنِي مُنْزَلًا مُبَارَكًا وَأَنْتَ خَيْرُ الْمُنْزِلِينَ
Transliteration
Rabbi anzilnee munzalan mubaarakan wa Anta khayrul-munzileen
Meaning
My Lord, let me land at a blessed landing place, and You are the best of those who accommodate.
Reference: Surah Al-Mu'minun 23:29
DUA 50

Travel

سُبْحَانَ الَّذِي سَخَّرَ لَنَا هَذَا وَمَا كُنَّا لَهُ مُقْرِنِينَ وَإِنَّا إِلَى رَبِّنَا لَمُنقَلِبُونَ
Transliteration
Subhaanalladhee sakhkhara lanaa haadhaa wa maa kunnaa lahu muqrineen, wa innaa ilaa Rabbinaa lamunqaliboon
Meaning
Glory is to Him who subjected this to us, and we could not have done it ourselves; and surely to our Lord we will return.
Reference: Surah Az-Zukhruf 43:13-14
DUA 51

Travel — Complete

اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّا نَسْأَلُكَ فِي سَفَرِنَا هَذَا الْبِرَّ وَالتَّقْوَى
Transliteration
Allaahumma innaa nas'aluka fee safarinaa haadhal-birra wat-taqwaa
Meaning
O Allah, we ask You on this journey of ours for righteousness and piety.
Reference: Sahih Muslim: 1342
DUA 52

Returning from Travel

آيِبُونَ تَائِبُونَ عَابِدُونَ لِرَبِّنَا حَامِدُونَ
Transliteration
Aayiboona taa'iboona 'aabidoona lirabbinaa haamidoon
Meaning
We return, repentant, worshipping our Lord and praising Him.
Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari: 1797
