DUA 85

When Facing Difficulty

اللَّهُمَّ لَا سَهْلَ إِلَّا مَا جَعَلْتَهُ سَهْلًا
Transliteration
Allaahumma laa sahla illaa maa ja'altahu sahlaa
Meaning
O Allah, there is no ease except what You make easy.
Reference: Ibn Hibban: 2427
