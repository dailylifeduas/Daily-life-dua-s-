Protection Duas

Daily Islamic supplications related to Protection, with Arabic, English transliteration, meaning and source references.

DUA 2

Everyday Dua - 2

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْجَنَّةَ وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ النَّارِ
Transliteration
Allaahumma 'innee 'as'alukal-jannata wa 'a'oothu bika minan-naar
Meaning
O Allah, I ask You for Paradise and seek Your protection from the Fire.
Reference: Abu Dawud: 792
DUA 28

For Help

رَبِّ أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ هَمَزَاتِ الشَّيَاطِينِ وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ رَبِّ أَنْ يَحْضُرُونِ
Transliteration
Rabbi a'oodhu bika min hamazaatish-shayaateen, wa a'oodhu bika rabbi an yahduroon
Meaning
My Lord, I seek refuge in You from the incitements of the devils, and I seek refuge in You, my Lord, lest they come near me.
Reference: Surah Al-Mu'minun 23:97-98
DUA 33

For Protection

أَعُوذُ بِكَلِمَاتِ اللَّهِ التَّامَّاتِ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ
Transliteration
A'oodhu bikalimaatillaahit-taammaati min sharri maa khalaq
Meaning
I seek refuge in the perfect words of Allah from the evil of what He has created.
Reference: Sahih Muslim: 2708
DUA 62

After Tashahhud — Protection

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ عَذَابِ جَهَنَّمَ وَمِنْ عَذَابِ الْقَبْرِ وَمِنْ فِتْنَةِ الْمَحْيَا وَالْمَمَاتِ وَمِنْ شَرِّ فِتْنَةِ الْمَسِيحِ الدَّجَّالِ
Transliteration
Allaahumma innee a'oodhu bika min 'adhaabi jahannam wa min 'adhaabil-qabr, wa min fitnatil-mahyaa wal-mamaat, wa min sharri fitnatil-maseehid-dajjaal
Meaning
O Allah, I seek refuge in You from the punishment of Hell, the punishment of the grave, the trials of life and death, and the evil trial of the False Messiah.
Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari: 1377
DUA 67

Protection from Harm

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ زَوَالِ نِعْمَتِكَ وَتَحَوُّلِ عَافِيَتِكَ وَفُجَاءَةِ نِقْمَتِكَ وَجَمِيعِ سَخَطِكَ
Transliteration
Allaahumma innee a'oodhu bika min zawaali ni'matika wa tahawwuli 'aafiyatika wa fujaa'ati niqmatika wa jamee'i sakhatik
Meaning
O Allah, I seek refuge in You from the removal of Your blessing, the change of Your well-being, the suddenness of Your punishment, and all of Your displeasure.
Reference: Sahih Muslim: 2739
DUA 72

For a Good End

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ سُوءِ الْأَخْلَاقِ وَالْأَعْمَالِ وَالْأَهْوَاءِ
Transliteration
Allaahumma innee a'oodhu bika min soo'il-akhlaaqi wal-a'maali wal-ahwaa'
Meaning
O Allah, I seek refuge in You from bad character, bad deeds and evil desires.
Reference: Jami` at-Tirmidhi: 3591
DUA 77

For Protection from Shirk

رَبِّ أَعُوذُ بِكَ أَنْ أَسْأَلَكَ مَا لَيْسَ لِي بِهِ عِلْمٌ
Transliteration
Rabbi a'oodhu bika an as'alaka maa laysa lee bihi 'ilm
Meaning
My Lord, I seek refuge in You from asking You for that of which I have no knowledge.
Reference: Surah Hud 11:47
DUA 87

For Safety

اللَّهُمَّ اسْتُرْ عَوْرَاتِي وَآمِنْ رَوْعَاتِي
Transliteration
Allaahummastur 'awraatee wa aamin raw'aatee
Meaning
O Allah, conceal my faults and calm my fears.
Reference: Sunan Abi Dawud: 5074
DUA 88

For Good in Affairs

رَبَّنَا لَا تَجْعَلْنَا فِتْنَةً لِلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا وَاغْفِرْ لَنَا رَبَّنَا إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ الْعَزِيزُ الْحَكِيمُ
Transliteration
Rabbanaa laa taj'alnaa fitnatan lilladheena kafaroo waghfir lanaa Rabbanaa innaka Antal-'Azeezul-Hakeem
Meaning
Our Lord, do not make us a trial for those who disbelieve, and forgive us, our Lord. Indeed, You are the Exalted in Might, the Wise.
Reference: Surah Al-Mumtahanah 60:5
DUA 98

During Strong Wind

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ خَيْرَهَا وَخَيْرَ مَا فِيهَا وَخَيْرَ مَا أُرْسِلَتْ بِهِ
Transliteration
Allaahumma innee as'aluka khayrahaa wa khayra maa feehaa wa khayra maa ursilat bih
Meaning
O Allah, I ask You for its good, the good within it, and the good with which it was sent.
Reference: Sahih Muslim: 899
DUA 107

When Seeing Something Pleasing

مَا شَاءَ اللَّهُ، لَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ
Transliteration
Maa shaa' Allaahu, laa quwwata illaa billaah
Meaning
What Allah has willed; there is no power except through Allah.
Reference: Surah Al-Kahf 18:39
DUA 109

For Fear of Evil

اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّا نَجْعَلُكَ فِي نُحُورِهِمْ وَنَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شُرُورِهِمْ
Transliteration
Allaahumma innaa naj'aluka fee nuhoorihim wa na'oodhu bika min shuroorihim
Meaning
O Allah, we place You against them and seek refuge in You from their evil.
Reference: Sunan Abi Dawud: 1537

DUA 116

For Protection from Old Age

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ أَرْذَلِ الْعُمُرِ

DUA 153

Dua for Justice

رَبَّنَا لَا تَجْعَلْنَا مَعَ الْقَوْمِ الظَّالِمِينَ
Transliteration
Rabbanaa laa taj'alnaa ma'al-qawmiz-zaalimeen
Meaning

DUA 155

Dua for Victory

رَبَّنَا لَا تَجْعَلْنَا فِتْنَةً لِلْقَوْمِ الظَّالِمِينَ وَنَجِّنَا بِرَحْمَتِكَ
Transliteration
Rabbanaa laa taj'alnaa fitnatan lil-qawmiz-zaalimeen wa najjinaa birahmatik
Meaning
Our Lord, do not make us a trial for the wrongdoing people and save us by Your mercy.
Reference: Surah Yunus 10:85-86

Dua for Forgiveness at Night

وَالَّذِينَ يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَا اصْرِفْ عَنَّا عَذَابَ جَهَنَّمَ
