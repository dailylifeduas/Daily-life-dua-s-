Patience Duas

Daily Islamic supplications related to Patience, with Arabic, English transliteration, meaning and source references.

DUA 27

For Patience

رَبَّنَا أَفْرِغْ عَلَيْنَا صَبْرًا وَتَوَفَّنَا مُسْلِمِينَ
Transliteration
Rabbanaa afrigh 'alaynaa sabran wa tawaffanaa muslimin
Meaning
Our Lord, pour upon us patience and let us die as Muslims in submission to You.
Reference: Surah Al-A'raf 7:126
DUA 128

Dua for Patience

رَبَّنَا أَفْرِغْ عَلَيْنَا صَبْرًا وَثَبِّتْ أَقْدَامَنَا
Transliteration
Rabbanaa afrigh 'alaynaa sabran wa thabbit aqdaamanaa
Meaning
Our Lord, pour upon us patience and make our feet firm.
Reference: Surah Al-Baqarah 2:250
