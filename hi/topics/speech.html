Speech Duas

Daily Islamic supplications related to Speech, with Arabic, English transliteration, meaning and source references.

DUA 26

For Eloquence

وَاحْلُلْ عُقْدَةً مِنْ لِسَانِي يَفْقَهُوا قَوْلِي
Transliteration
Wahlul 'uqdatan min lisaanee yafqahoo qawlee
Meaning
And untie the knot from my tongue so that they may understand my speech.
Reference: Surah Ta-Ha 20:27-28
