Family Duas

Daily Islamic supplications related to Family, with Arabic, English transliteration, meaning and source references.

DUA 23

For Righteous Family

رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَامًا
Transliteration
Rabbanaa hab lanaa min azwaajinaa wa dhurriyyaatinaa qurrata a'yunin waj'alnaa lil-muttaqeena imaamaa
Meaning
Our Lord, grant us from our spouses and offspring comfort to our eyes and make us an example for the righteous.
Reference: Surah Al-Furqan 25:74
DUA 122

Dua for Righteousness

رَبِّ هَبْ لِي مِنْ لَدُنْكَ ذُرِّيَّةً طَيِّبَةً
Transliteration
Rabbi hab lee mil-ladunka dhurriyyatan tayyibah
Meaning
My Lord, grant me from You a good offspring.
Reference: Surah Aal-e-Imran 3:38
DUA 150

Dua for Parents and Good Deeds

وَأَصْلِحْ لِي فِي ذُرِّيَّتِي
Transliteration
Wa aslih lee fee dhurriyyatee
Meaning
And make my offspring righteous for me.
Reference: Surah Al-Ahqaf 46:15

DUA 180

Dua for a Good Offspring

رَبِّ لَا تَذَرْنِي فَرْدًا وَأَنْتَ خَيْرُ الْوَارِثِينَ
Transliteration
Rabbi laa tadharni fardan wa Anta khayrul-waaritheen
Meaning
My Lord, do not leave me alone, and You are the best of inheritors.
Reference: Surah Al-Anbiya 21:89
