DUA 25

For Ease

رَبِّ اشْرَحْ لِي صَدْرِي وَيَسِّرْ لِي أَمْرِي
Transliteration
Rabbishrah lee sadree wa yassir lee amree
Meaning
My Lord, expand for me my chest and ease for me my task.
Reference: Surah Ta-Ha 20:25-26
