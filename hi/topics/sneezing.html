Sneezing Duas

Daily Islamic supplications related to Sneezing, with Arabic, English transliteration, meaning and source references.

DUA 53

When Sneezing

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ
Transliteration
Alhamdu lillaah
Meaning
All praise is for Allah.
Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari: 6224
DUA 54

Reply to Sneezing

يَرْحَمُكَ اللَّهُ
Transliteration
Yarhamukallaah
Meaning
May Allah have mercy on you.
Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari: 6224
DUA 55

Response to Reply

يَهْدِيكُمُ اللَّهُ وَيُصْلِحُ بَالَكُمْ
Transliteration
Yahdeekumullaahu wa yuslihu baalakum
Meaning
May Allah guide you and rectify your affairs.
Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari: 6224
