Help Duas

Daily Islamic supplications related to Help, with Arabic, English transliteration, meaning and source references.

DUA 75

For Good Deeds

رَبِّ أَعِنِّي وَلَا تُعِنْ عَلَيَّ، وَانْصُرْنِي وَلَا تَنْصُرْ عَلَيَّ
Transliteration
Rabbi a'innee wa laa tu'in 'alayya, wansurnee wa laa tansur 'alayya
Meaning
My Lord, help me and do not help against me; support me and do not support against me.
Reference: Jami` at-Tirmidhi: 3551
DUA 119

For Allah's Help

إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ
Transliteration
Iyyaaka na'budu wa iyyaaka nasta'een
Meaning
You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help.
Reference: Surah Al-Fatihah 1:5
DUA 129

Dua for Victory

وَانْصُرْنَا عَلَى الْقَوْمِ الْكَافِرِينَ
Transliteration
Wansurnaa 'alal-qawmil-kaafireen
Meaning
And grant us victory over the disbelieving people.
Reference: Surah Al-Baqarah 2:250
DUA 135

Dua for Help

أَنْتَ مَوْلَانَا فَانْصُرْنَا عَلَى الْقَوْمِ الْكَافِرِينَ
Transliteration
Anta mawlaanaa fansurnaa 'alal-qawmil-kaafireen
Meaning
You are our Protector, so grant us victory over the disbelieving people.
Reference: Surah Al-Baqarah 2:286
DUA 189

Dua for Authority and Help

وَاجْعَلْ لِي مِنْ لَدُنْكَ سُلْطَانًا نَصِيرًا
Transliteration
Waj'al lee mil-ladunka sultaanan naseeraa
Meaning
And grant me from Yourself a supporting authority.
Reference: Surah Al-Isra 17:80
