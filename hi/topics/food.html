Food Duas

Daily Islamic supplications related to Food, with Arabic, English transliteration, meaning and source references.

DUA 9

Before Eating

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ
Transliteration
Bismillaah
Meaning
In the name of Allah.
Reference: Sunan Abi Dawud: 3767
DUA 10

If You Forget Before Eating

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ فِي أَوَّلِهِ وَآخِرِهِ
Transliteration
Bismillaahi fee awwalihi wa aakhirihi
Meaning
In the name of Allah at its beginning and at its end.
Reference: Sunan Abi Dawud: 3767
DUA 11

After Eating

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَطْعَمَنِي هَذَا وَرَزَقَنِيهِ مِنْ غَيْرِ حَوْلٍ مِنِّي وَلَا قُوَّةٍ
Transliteration
Alhamdu lillaahil-ladhee at'amanee haadhaa wa razaqaneehi min ghayri hawlin minnee wa laa quwwah
Meaning
All praise is for Allah who fed me this and provided it for me without any strength or power from me.
Reference: Sunan Abi Dawud: 4023
