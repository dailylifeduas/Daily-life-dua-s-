Guidance Duas

Daily Islamic supplications related to Guidance, with Arabic, English transliteration, meaning and source references.

DUA 22

For Steadfastness

رَبَّنَا لَا تُزِغْ قُلُوبَنَا بَعْدَ إِذْ هَدَيْتَنَا وَهَبْ لَنَا مِنْ لَدُنْكَ رَحْمَةً إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ الْوَهَّابُ
Transliteration
Rabbanaa laa tuzigh quloobanaa ba'da idh hadaytanaa wa hab lanaa mil-ladunka rahmah, innaka antal-wahhaab
Meaning
Our Lord, do not let our hearts deviate after You have guided us, and grant us mercy from You. Indeed, You are the Bestower.
Reference: Surah Aal-e-Imran 3:8
DUA 29

For Mercy

رَبَّنَا آتِنَا مِنْ لَدُنْكَ رَحْمَةً وَهَيِّئْ لَنَا مِنْ أَمْرِنَا رَشَدًا
Transliteration
Rabbanaa aatinaa mil-ladunka rahmatan wa hayyi' lanaa min amrinaa rashadaa
Meaning
Our Lord, grant us mercy from Yourself and guide us to the right course in our affair.
Reference: Surah Al-Kahf 18:10
DUA 39

For Guidance

اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ
Transliteration
Ihdinas-siraatal-mustaqeem
Meaning
Guide us to the Straight Path.
Reference: Surah Al-Fatihah 1:6
DUA 78

For a Good Character and Actions

اللَّهُمَّ اهْدِنِي وَسَدِّدْنِي
Transliteration
Allaahummah-dinee wa saddidnee
Meaning
O Allah, guide me and make me steadfast and correct.
Reference: Sahih Muslim: 2725
DUA 94

Istikhara — Seeking Allah’s Choice

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْتَخِيرُكَ بِعِلْمِكَ، وَأَسْتَقْدِرُكَ بِقُدْرَتِكَ
Transliteration
Allaahumma innee astakheeruka bi'ilmika, wa astaqdiruka bi-qudratika
Meaning
O Allah, I seek Your guidance by Your knowledge, and I seek ability by Your power.
Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari: 1166

DUA 188

Dua for Good in the Hereafter

رَبِّ أَدْخِلْنِي مُدْخَلَ صِدْقٍ وَأَخْرِجْنِي مُخْرَجَ صِدْقٍ
Transliteration
Rabbi adkhilnee mudkhala sidqin wa akhrijnee mukhraja sidq
Meaning
My Lord, cause me to enter a sound entrance and to exit a sound exit.
Reference: Surah Al-Isra 17:80
DUA 195

Dua for Right Guidance

وَهَيِّئْ لَنَا مِنْ أَمْرِنَا رَشَدًا
Transliteration
Wa hayyi' lanaa min amrinaa rashadaa
Meaning
And prepare for us from our affair right guidance.
Reference: Surah Al-Kahf 18:10
DUA 199

Dua for Rescue

رَبِّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ أَنْ أَسْأَلَكَ مَا لَيْسَ لِي بِهِ عِلْمٌ
Transliteration
Rabbi innee a'oodhu bika an as'alaka maa laysa lee bihi 'ilm
Meaning
My Lord, I seek refuge in You from asking that of which I have no knowledge.
Reference: Surah Hud 11:47
DUA 203

Dua for a Good Place to Stay

وَقُلْ رَبِّ أَدْخِلْنِي مُدْخَلَ صِدْقٍ
Transliteration
Wa qul rabbi adkhilnee mudkhala sidq
Meaning
And say: My Lord, cause me to enter a sound entrance.
Reference: Surah Al-Isra 17:80
