Distress Duas

Daily Islamic supplications related to Distress, with Arabic, English transliteration, meaning and source references.

DUA 30

Dua of Prophet Yunus

لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ سُبْحَانَكَ إِنِّي كُنْتُ مِنَ الظَّالِمِينَ
Transliteration
Laa ilaaha illaa Anta subhaanaka innee kuntu minaz-zaalimeen
Meaning
There is no deity except You; glory is to You. Indeed, I have been among the wrongdoers.
Reference: Surah Al-Anbiya 21:87
DUA 36

For Anxiety

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْهَمِّ وَالْحَزَنِ
Transliteration
Allaahumma innee a'oodhu bika minal-hammi wal-hazan
Meaning
O Allah, I seek refuge in You from anxiety and grief.
Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari: 6369
DUA 93

For Relief

أَمَّنْ يُجِيبُ الْمُضْطَرَّ إِذَا دَعَاهُ وَيَكْشِفُ السُّوءَ
Transliteration
Amman yujeebul-mudtarra idhaa da'aahu wa yakshifus-soo'
Meaning
Who responds to the desperate one when he calls upon Him and removes the evil?
Reference: Surah An-Naml 27:62
