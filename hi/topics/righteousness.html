Righteousness Duas

Daily Islamic supplications related to Righteousness, with Arabic, English transliteration, meaning and source references.

DUA 112

For a Good Outcome

وَالْعَاقِبَةُ لِلتَّقْوَى
Transliteration
Wal-'aaqibatu lit-taqwaa
Meaning
The good outcome is for righteousness.
Reference: Surah Ta-Ha 20:132
DUA 146

Dua for Goodness

وَتَوَفَّنَا مَعَ الْأَبْرَارِ
Transliteration
Wa tawaffanaa ma'al-abraar
Meaning
And cause us to die with the righteous.
Reference: Surah Aal-e-Imran 3:193
DUA 174

Dua to Be an Example

وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَامًا
Transliteration
Waj'alnaa lil-muttaqeena imaamaa
Meaning
And make us an example for the righteous.
Reference: Surah Al-Furqan 25:74
