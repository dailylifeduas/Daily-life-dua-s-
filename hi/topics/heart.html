DUA 113

For Righteousness

رَبِّ أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ عِلْمٍ لَا يَنْفَعُ وَمِنْ قَلْبٍ لَا يَخْشَعُ
Transliteration
Rabbi a'oodhu bika min 'ilmin laa yanfa' wa min qalbin laa yakhsha'
Meaning
My Lord, I seek refuge in You from knowledge that does not benefit and a heart that does not humble itself.
Reference: Sunan an-Nasa'i: 5536
