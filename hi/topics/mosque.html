Mosque Duas

Daily Islamic supplications related to Mosque, with Arabic, English transliteration, meaning and source references.

DUA 16

Entering Mosque

اللَّهُمَّ افْتَحْ لِي أَبْوَابَ رَحْمَتِكَ
Transliteration
Allaahumma iftah lee abwaaba rahmatik
Meaning
O Allah, open for me the doors of Your mercy.
Reference: Sahih Muslim: 713
DUA 17

Leaving Mosque

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ مِنْ فَضْلِكَ
Transliteration
Allaahumma innee as'aluka min fadlik
Meaning
O Allah, I ask You from Your bounty.
Reference: Sahih Muslim: 713
