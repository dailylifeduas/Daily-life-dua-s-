DUA 101

Breaking the Fast

ذَهَبَ الظَّمَأُ وَابْتَلَّتِ الْعُرُوقُ وَثَبَتَ الْأَجْرُ إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ
Transliteration
Dhahabaz-zama'u wabtallatil-'urooqu wa thabatal-ajru in shaa'allaah
Meaning
The thirst has gone, the veins are moistened, and the reward is assured, if Allah wills.
Reference: Sunan Abi Dawud: 2357
