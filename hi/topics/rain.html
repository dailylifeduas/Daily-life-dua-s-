Rain Duas

Daily Islamic supplications related to Rain, with Arabic, English transliteration, meaning and source references.

DUA 96

For Rain

اللَّهُمَّ صَيِّبًا نَافِعًا
Transliteration
Allaahumma sayyiban naafi'an
Meaning
O Allah, make it beneficial rain.
Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari: 1032
DUA 97

After Rain

مُطِرْنَا بِفَضْلِ اللَّهِ وَرَحْمَتِهِ
Transliteration
Mutirnaa bifadlillaahi wa rahmatih
Meaning
We have been given rain by the grace and mercy of Allah.
Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari: 846
