Funeral Duas

Daily Islamic supplications related to Funeral, with Arabic, English transliteration, meaning and source references.

DUA 103

For the Deceased

اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لَهُ وَارْحَمْهُ وَعَافِهِ وَاعْفُ عَنْهُ
Transliteration
Allaahummaghfir lahu warhamhu wa 'aafihi wa'fu 'anhu
Meaning
O Allah, forgive him, have mercy on him, grant him well-being, and pardon him.
Reference: Sahih Muslim: 963
DUA 105

Condolence

إِنَّ لِلَّهِ مَا أَخَذَ وَلَهُ مَا أَعْطَى وَكُلٌّ عِنْدَهُ بِأَجَلٍ مُسَمًّى فَلْتَصْبِرْ وَلْتَحْتَسِبْ
Transliteration
Inna lillaahi maa akhadha wa lahu maa a'taa wa kullun 'indahu bi-ajalin musamman faltasbir waltahtasib
Meaning
Indeed, to Allah belongs what He took and to Him belongs what He gave, and everything has an appointed term, so be patient and hope for reward.
Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari: 1284
