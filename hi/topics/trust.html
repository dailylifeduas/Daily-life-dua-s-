Trust Duas

Daily Islamic supplications related to Trust, with Arabic, English transliteration, meaning and source references.

DUA 34

For Fear

حَسْبُنَا اللَّهُ وَنِعْمَ الْوَكِيلُ
Transliteration
Hasbunallaahu wa ni'mal-wakeel
Meaning
Allah is Sufficient for us, and He is the best Disposer of affairs.
Reference: Surah Aal-e-Imran 3:173
DUA 35

For Trust

حَسْبِيَ اللَّهُ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا هُوَ عَلَيْهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ
Transliteration
Hasbiyallaahu laa ilaaha illaa Huwa, 'alayhi tawakkaltu
Meaning
Allah is sufficient for me. There is no deity except Him. Upon Him I rely.
Reference: Surah At-Tawbah 9:129
DUA 156

Dua for Rescue

رَبَّنَا عَلَيْكَ تَوَكَّلْنَا وَإِلَيْكَ أَنَبْنَا وَإِلَيْكَ الْمَصِيرُ
Transliteration
Rabbanaa 'alayka tawakkalnaa wa ilayka anabnaa wa ilaykal-maseer
Meaning
Our Lord, upon You we have relied, and to You we have returned, and to You is the destination.
Reference: Surah Al-Mumtahanah 60:4
