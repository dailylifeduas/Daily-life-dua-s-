Acceptance Duas

Daily Islamic supplications related to Acceptance, with Arabic, English transliteration, meaning and source references.

DUA 41

For Acceptance

رَبَّنَا تَقَبَّلْ مِنَّا إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ
Transliteration
Rabbanaa taqabbal minnaa innaka Antas-Samee'ul-'Aleem
Meaning
Our Lord, accept from us. Indeed, You are the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing.
Reference: Surah Al-Baqarah 2:127
