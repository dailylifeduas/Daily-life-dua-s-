DUA 14

Entering Home

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ وَلَجْنَا، وَبِسْمِ اللَّهِ خَرَجْنَا، وَعَلَى اللَّهِ رَبِّنَا تَوَكَّلْنَا
Transliteration
Bismillaahi walajnaa, wa bismillaahi kharajnaa, wa 'alallaahi rabbinaa tawakkalnaa
Meaning
In the name of Allah we enter, in the name of Allah we leave, and upon our Lord Allah we rely.
Reference: Sunan Abi Dawud: 5096
