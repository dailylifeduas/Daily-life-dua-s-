Mercy Duas

Daily Islamic supplications related to Mercy, with Arabic, English transliteration, meaning and source references.

DUA 121

Dua for Mercy

رَبَّنَا آتِنَا مِنْ لَدُنْكَ رَحْمَةً وَهَيِّئْ لَنَا مِنْ أَمْرِنَا رَشَدًا
Transliteration
Rabbanaa aatinaa mil-ladunka rahmatan wa hayyi' lanaa min amrinaa rashadaa
Meaning
Our Lord, grant us mercy from Yourself and guide us to the right course in our affair.
Reference: Surah Al-Kahf 18:10
DUA 131

Dua for Not Being Burdened

رَبَّنَا لَا تُؤَاخِذْنَا إِنْ نَسِينَا أَوْ أَخْطَأْنَا
Transliteration
Rabbanaa laa tu'aakhidhnaa in naseenaa aw akhta'naa
Meaning
Our Lord, do not impose blame upon us if we forget or make a mistake.
Reference: Surah Al-Baqarah 2:286
DUA 134

Dua for Mercy

وَاعْفُ عَنَّا وَاغْفِرْ لَنَا وَارْحَمْنَا
Transliteration
Wa'fu 'annaa waghfir lanaa warhamnaa
Meaning
Pardon us, forgive us, and have mercy upon us.
Reference: Surah Al-Baqarah 2:286
DUA 137

Dua for Mercy from Allah

وَهَبْ لَنَا مِنْ لَدُنْكَ رَحْمَةً
Transliteration
Wa hab lanaa mil-ladunka rahmah
Meaning
And grant us mercy from You.
Reference: Surah Aal-e-Imran 3:8
DUA 159

Dua for Trust and Mercy

رَبَّنَا عَلَيْكَ تَوَكَّلْنَا فَاغْفِرْ لَنَا وَارْحَمْنَا
Transliteration
Rabbanaa 'alayka tawakkalnaa faghfir lanaa warhamnaa
Meaning
Our Lord, upon You we have relied, so forgive us and have mercy upon us.
Reference: Surah Al-Mumtahanah 60:4-5
DUA 176

Dua for Mercy

رَبِّ اغْفِرْ وَارْحَمْ وَأَنْتَ خَيْرُ الرَّاحِمِينَ
Transliteration
Rabbighfir warham wa Anta khayrur-raahimeen
Meaning
My Lord, forgive and have mercy, and You are the best of the merciful.
Reference: Surah Al-Mu'minun 23:118
DUA 194

Dua for Good Residence

رَبَّنَا وَأَدْخِلْنَا فِي رَحْمَتِكَ
Transliteration
Rabbanaa wa adkhilnaa fee rahmatik
Meaning
Our Lord, admit us into Your mercy.
Reference: Surah Al-Kahf 18:10
DUA 208

Dua for Forgiveness and Faith

رَبَّنَا آمَنَّا فَاغْفِرْ لَنَا وَارْحَمْنَا
Transliteration
Rabbanaa aamannaa faghfir lanaa warhamnaa
Meaning
Our Lord, we have believed, so forgive us and have mercy upon us.
Reference: Surah Al-Mu'minun 23:109
DUA 210

Dua for Mercy and Knowledge

رَبَّنَا وَسِعْتَ كُلَّ شَيْءٍ رَحْمَةً وَعِلْمًا فَاغْفِرْ لِلَّذِينَ تَابُوا
Transliteration
Rabbanaa wasi'ta kulla shay'in rahmatan wa 'ilman faghfir lilladheena taaboo
Meaning
Our Lord, You encompass all things in mercy and knowledge, so forgive those who repent.
Reference: Surah Ghafir 40:7
