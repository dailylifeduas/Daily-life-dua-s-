Hereafter Duas

Daily Islamic supplications related to Hereafter, with Arabic, English transliteration, meaning and source references.

DUA 43

For a Righteous Death

تَوَفَّنِي مُسْلِمًا وَأَلْحِقْنِي بِالصَّالِحِينَ
Transliteration
Tawaffanee musliman wa alhiqnee bis-saaliheen
Meaning
Cause me to die as a Muslim and join me with the righteous.
Reference: Surah Yusuf 12:101
DUA 70

For Paradise

رَبَّنَا وَآتِنَا مَا وَعَدْتَنَا عَلَى رُسُلِكَ وَلَا تُخْزِنَا يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ
Transliteration
Rabbanaa wa aatinaa maa wa'adtanaa 'alaa rusulika wa laa tukhzinaa yawm al-qiyaamah
Meaning
Our Lord, grant us what You promised us through Your messengers and do not disgrace us on the Day of Resurrection.
Reference: Surah Aal-e-Imran 3:194
DUA 138

Dua for Certainty

رَبَّنَا إِنَّكَ جَامِعُ النَّاسِ لِيَوْمٍ لَا رَيْبَ فِيهِ
Transliteration
Rabbanaa innaka jaami'un-naasi li-yawmin laa rayba feeh
Meaning
Our Lord, surely You will gather the people for a Day about which there is no doubt.
Reference: Surah Aal-e-Imran 3:9
DUA 142

Dua for Mercy After Hardship

رَبَّنَا مَا خَلَقْتَ هَذَا بَاطِلًا سُبْحَانَكَ فَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ
Transliteration
Rabbanaa maa khalaqta haadhaa baatilan subhaanaka faqinaa 'adhaaban-naar
Meaning
Our Lord, You did not create this aimlessly; glory be to You, so protect us from the punishment of the Fire.
Reference: Surah Aal-e-Imran 3:191
DUA 143

Dua for Sins to Be Forgiven

رَبَّنَا إِنَّكَ مَنْ تُدْخِلِ النَّارَ فَقَدْ أَخْزَيْتَهُ
Transliteration
Rabbanaa innaka man tudkhilin-naara faqad akhzaytah
Meaning
Our Lord, indeed whoever You admit to the Fire, You have disgraced him.
Reference: Surah Aal-e-Imran 3:192

DUA 160

Dua for Light

رَبَّنَا أَتْمِمْ لَنَا نُورَنَا وَاغْفِرْ لَنَا
Transliteration
Rabbanaa atmim lanaa nooranaa waghfir lanaa
Meaning
Our Lord, perfect for us our light and forgive us.
Reference: Surah At-Tahrim 66:8
