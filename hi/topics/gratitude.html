Gratitude Duas

Daily Islamic supplications related to Gratitude, with Arabic, English transliteration, meaning and source references.

DUA 68

For Gratitude

رَبِّ أَوْزِعْنِي أَنْ أَشْكُرَ نِعْمَتَكَ الَّتِي أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيَّ
Transliteration
Rabbi awzi'nee an ashkura ni'mataka allatee an'amta 'alayya
Meaning
My Lord, enable me to be grateful for Your favor which You have bestowed upon me.
Reference: Surah An-Naml 27:19
DUA 84

For Someone Who Does Good

جَزَاكَ اللَّهُ خَيْرًا
Transliteration
Jazaakallaahu khayran
Meaning
May Allah reward you with goodness.
Reference: Jami` at-Tirmidhi: 2035

DUA 197

Dua for Victory and Forgiveness

رَبِّ قَدْ آتَيْتَنِي مِنَ الْمُلْكِ وَعَلَّمْتَنِي مِنْ تَأْوِيلِ الْأَحَادِيثِ
Transliteration

Meaning

Reference: Surah Yusuf 12:101
