DUA 108

When Angry

أَعُوذُ بِاللَّهِ مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ الرَّجِيمِ
Transliteration
A'oodhu billaahi minash-shaytaanir-rajeem
Meaning
I seek refuge in Allah from Satan, the accursed.
Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari: 3282
