Prayer Duas

Daily Islamic supplications related to Prayer, with Arabic, English transliteration, meaning and source references.

DUA 56

At the Beginning of Prayer

سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ وَبِحَمْدِكَ وَتَبَارَكَ اسْمُكَ وَتَعَالَى جَدُّكَ وَلَا إِلَهَ غَيْرُكَ
Transliteration
Subhaanaka Allaahumma wa bihamdika wa tabaarakasmuka wa ta'aalaa jadduka wa laa ilaaha ghayruk
Meaning
Glory is to You, O Allah, and praise. Blessed is Your name, exalted is Your majesty, and there is no deity besides You.
Reference: Sunan Abi Dawud: 775
DUA 57

Ruku

سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْعَظِيمِ
Transliteration
Subhaana Rabbiyal-'Azeem
Meaning
Glory is to my Lord, the Most Great.
Reference: Sahih Muslim: 772
DUA 58

Sujood

سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْأَعْلَى
Transliteration
Subhaana Rabbiyal-A'laa
Meaning
Glory is to my Lord, the Most High.
Reference: Sahih Muslim: 772
DUA 59

Between Two Sujood

رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي
Transliteration
Rabbighfir lee
Meaning
My Lord, forgive me.
Reference: Sunan Abi Dawud: 874
DUA 60

After Ruku

رَبَّنَا وَلَكَ الْحَمْدُ
Transliteration
Rabbanaa wa lakal-hamd
Meaning
Our Lord, and to You is all praise.
Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari: 789
DUA 61

Tashahhud

التَّحِيَّاتُ لِلَّهِ وَالصَّلَوَاتُ وَالطَّيِّبَاتُ
Transliteration
At-tahiyyaatu lillaahi was-salawaatu wat-tayyibaat
Meaning
All greetings, prayers and pure words are for Allah.
Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari: 831
DUA 89

For Guidance of Family

رَبِّ اجْعَلْنِي مُقِيمَ الصَّلَاةِ وَمِنْ ذُرِّيَّتِي
Transliteration
Rabbij'alnee muqeemas-salaati wa min dhurriyyatee
Meaning
My Lord, make me an establisher of prayer, and many from my descendants.
Reference: Surah Ibrahim 14:40
DUA 90

For Acceptance of Prayer

رَبَّنَا وَتَقَبَّلْ دُعَاءِ
Transliteration
Rabbanaa wa taqabbal du'aa
Meaning
Our Lord, and accept my supplication.
Reference: Surah Ibrahim 14:40
