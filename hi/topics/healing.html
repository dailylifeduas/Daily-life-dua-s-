DUA 191

Dua for Healing

وَنُنَزِّلُ مِنَ الْقُرْآنِ مَا هُوَ شِفَاءٌ
Transliteration
Wa nunazzilu minal-Qur'aani maa huwa shifaa'
Meaning
And We send down of the Qur'an that which is healing.
Reference: Surah Al-Isra 17:82
