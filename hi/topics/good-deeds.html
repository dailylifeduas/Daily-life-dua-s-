Good Deeds Duas

Daily Islamic supplications related to Good Deeds, with Arabic, English transliteration, meaning and source references.

DUA 149

Dua for Righteous Deeds

وَأَنْ أَعْمَلَ صَالِحًا تَرْضَاهُ
Transliteration
Wa an a'mala saalihan tardaah
Meaning
And to do righteousness of which You approve.
Reference: Surah An-Naml 27:19
