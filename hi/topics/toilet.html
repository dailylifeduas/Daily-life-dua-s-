Toilet Duas

Daily Islamic supplications related to Toilet, with Arabic, English transliteration, meaning and source references.

DUA 7

Before Entering Toilet

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْخُبُثِ وَالْخَبَائِثِ
Transliteration
Allaahumma innee a'oodhu bika minal-khubthi wal-khabaa'ith
Meaning
O Allah, I seek refuge in You from the male and female devils.
Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari: 142
DUA 8

After Leaving Toilet

غُفْرَانَكَ
Transliteration
Ghufraanak
Meaning
I seek Your forgiveness.
Reference: Sunan Abi Dawud: 30
