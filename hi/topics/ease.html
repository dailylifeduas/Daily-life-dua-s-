Ease Duas

Daily Islamic supplications related to Ease, with Arabic, English transliteration, meaning and source references.

DUA 132

Dua for Ease

رَبَّنَا وَلَا تَحْمِلْ عَلَيْنَا إِصْرًا كَمَا حَمَلْتَهُ عَلَى الَّذِينَ مِنْ قَبْلِنَا
Transliteration
Rabbanaa wa laa tahmil 'alaynaa isran kamaa hamaltahu 'alal-ladheena min qablinaa
Meaning
Our Lord, do not burden us with a burden as You burdened those before us.
Reference: Surah Al-Baqarah 2:286
DUA 163

Dua for Ease and Speech

رَبِّ اشْرَحْ لِي صَدْرِي
Transliteration
Rabbishrah lee sadree
Meaning
My Lord, expand for me my chest.
Reference: Surah Ta-Ha 20:25
DUA 164

Dua for Ease in Task

وَيَسِّرْ لِي أَمْرِي
Transliteration
Wa yassir lee amree
Meaning
And ease for me my task.
Reference: Surah Ta-Ha 20:26
