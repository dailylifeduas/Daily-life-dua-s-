Knowledge Duas

Daily Islamic supplications related to Knowledge, with Arabic, English transliteration, meaning and source references.

DUA 18

For Knowledge

رَبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا
Transliteration
Rabbi zidnee 'ilmaa
Meaning
My Lord, increase me in knowledge.
Reference: Surah Ta-Ha 20:114
DUA 80

For Beneficial Knowledge

اللَّهُمَّ انْفَعْنِي بِمَا عَلَّمْتَنِي وَعَلِّمْنِي مَا يَنْفَعُنِي وَزِدْنِي عِلْمًا
Transliteration
Allaahummanfa'nee bimaa 'allamtanee wa 'allimnee maa yanfa'unee wa zidnee 'ilmaa
Meaning
O Allah, benefit me through what You have taught me, teach me what benefits me, and increase me in knowledge.
Reference: Jami` at-Tirmidhi: 3599
