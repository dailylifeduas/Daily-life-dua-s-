Dhikr Duas

Daily Islamic supplications related to Dhikr, with Arabic, English transliteration, meaning and source references.

DUA 3

Everyday Dua - 3

سُبْحَانَ اللهِ ، والْحَمْدُ للهِ ، وَاللهُ أَكْبَرُ
Transliteration
Subhaanallaahi, Walhamdu lillaahi, Wallaahu 'Akbar
Meaning
Glory is to Allah, praise is to Allah, Allah is the Most Great!
Reference: Muslim: 33:3813
DUA 106

Entering Market

لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ، يُحْيِي وَيُمِيتُ
Transliteration
Laa ilaaha illallaahu wahdahu laa shareeka lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-hamd, yuhyee wa yumeet
Meaning
There is no deity except Allah alone, without partner. His is the dominion and His is the praise; He gives life and causes death.
Reference: Jami` at-Tirmidhi: 3428
