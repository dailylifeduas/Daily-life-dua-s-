Character Duas

Daily Islamic supplications related to Character, with Arabic, English transliteration, meaning and source references.

DUA 45

For Good Character

اللَّهُمَّ اهْدِنِي لِأَحْسَنِ الْأَخْلَاقِ لَا يَهْدِي لِأَحْسَنِهَا إِلَّا أَنْتَ
Transliteration
Allaahumma-hdinee li-ahsanil-akhlaaqi laa yahdee li-ahsanihaa illaa Anta
Meaning
O Allah, guide me to the best character; none can guide to the best of it except You.
Reference: Sahih Muslim: 771
DUA 117

For Protection from Bad Morals

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ مُنْكَرَاتِ الْأَخْلَاقِ وَالْأَعْمَالِ وَالْأَهْوَاءِ
Transliteration
Allaahumma innee a'oodhu bika min munkaraatil-akhlaaqi wal-a'maali wal-ahwaa
Meaning
O Allah, I seek refuge in You from evil morals, deeds and desires.
Reference: Jami` at-Tirmidhi: 3591
DUA 158

Dua for Clean Hearts

وَلَا تَجْعَلْ فِي قُلُوبِنَا غِلًّا لِلَّذِينَ آمَنُوا
Transliteration
Wa laa taj'al fee quloobinaa ghillal-lilladheena aamanoo
Meaning
And do not put in our hearts any resentment toward those who have believed.
Reference: Surah Al-Hashr 59:10
