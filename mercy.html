DUA 121

Dua for Mercy

Quran • Mercy
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا مِنْ لَدُنْكَ رَحْمَةً وَهَيِّئْ لَنَا مِنْ أَمْرِنَا رَشَدًا
Transliteration
Rabbanaa aatinaa mil-ladunka rahmatan wa hayyi' lanaa min amrinaa rashadaa
Meaning
Our Lord, grant us mercy from Yourself and guide us to the right course in our affair.
Reference: Surah Al-Kahf 18:10
DUA 131

Dua for Not Being Burdened

Quran • Mercy
رَبَّنَا لَا تُؤَاخِذْنَا إِنْ نَسِينَا أَوْ أَخْطَأْنَا
Transliteration
Rabbanaa laa tu'aakhidhnaa in naseenaa aw akhta'naa
Meaning
Our Lord, do not impose blame upon us if we forget or make a mistake.
Reference: Surah Al-Baqarah 2:286

DUA 210

Dua for Mercy and Knowledge

Quran • Mercy
رَبَّنَا وَسِعْتَ كُلَّ شَيْءٍ رَحْمَةً وَعِلْمًا فَاغْفِرْ لِلَّذِينَ تَابُوا
Transliteration

Meaning

Reference: Surah Ghafir 40:7
