DUA 22

For Steadfastness

Faith • Guidance
رَبَّنَا لَا تُزِغْ قُلُوبَنَا بَعْدَ إِذْ هَدَيْتَنَا وَهَبْ لَنَا مِنْ لَدُنْكَ رَحْمَةً إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ الْوَهَّابُ
Transliteration
Rabbanaa laa tuzigh quloobanaa ba'da idh hadaytanaa wa hab lanaa mil-ladunka rahmah, innaka antal-wahhaab
Meaning
Our Lord, do not let our hearts deviate after You have guided us, and grant us mercy from You. Indeed, You are the Bestower.
Reference: Surah Aal-e-Imran 3:8
DUA 29

For Mercy

Mercy • Guidance
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا مِنْ لَدُنْكَ رَحْمَةً وَهَيِّئْ لَنَا مِنْ أَمْرِنَا رَشَدًا
Transliteration
Rabbanaa aatinaa mil-ladunka rahmatan wa hayyi' lanaa min amrinaa rashadaa
Meaning
Our Lord, grant us mercy from Yourself and guide us to the right course in our affair.
Reference: Surah Al-Kahf 18:10

DUA 94

Istikhara — Seeking Allah’s Choice

Istikhara • Guidance
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْتَخِيرُكَ بِعِلْمِكَ، وَأَسْتَقْدِرُكَ بِقُدْرَتِكَ
Transliteration
Allaahumma innee astakheeruka bi'ilmika, wa astaqdiruka bi-qudratika
Meaning
O Allah, I seek Your guidance by Your knowledge, and I seek ability by Your power.
Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari: 1166

DUA 188

Dua for Good in the Hereafter

Quran • Guidance
رَبِّ أَدْخِلْنِي مُدْخَلَ صِدْقٍ وَأَخْرِجْنِي مُخْرَجَ صِدْقٍ
Transliteration
Rabbi adkhilnee mudkhala sidqin wa akhrijnee mukhraja sidq
Meaning
My Lord, cause me to enter a sound entrance and to exit a sound exit.
Reference: Surah Al-Isra 17:80
