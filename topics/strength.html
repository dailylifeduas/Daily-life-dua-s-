DUA 82

For Strength

Dhikr • Strength
لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ
Transliteration
Laa hawla wa laa quwwata illaa billaah
Meaning
There is no power and no strength except with Allah.
Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari: 4205
