DUA 40

For a Good End

Patience • Faith
رَبَّنَا أَفْرِغْ عَلَيْنَا صَبْرًا وَتَوَفَّنَا مُسْلِمِينَ
Transliteration
Rabbanaa afrigh 'alaynaa sabran wa tawaffanaa muslimin
Meaning
Our Lord, pour upon us patience and let us die as Muslims.
Reference: Surah Al-A'raf 7:126
DUA 71

For Allah's Love

Faith
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ حُبَّكَ وَحُبَّ مَنْ يُحِبُّكَ وَالْعَمَلَ الَّذِي يُبَلِّغُنِي حُبَّكَ
Transliteration
Allaahumma innee as'aluka hubbaka wa hubba man yuhibbuka wal-'amalalladhee yuballighunee hubbak
Meaning
O Allah, I ask You for Your love, the love of those whom You love, and deeds that bring me closer to Your love.
Reference: Jami` at-Tirmidhi: 3490
DUA 124

Dua for Submission

Quran • Faith
رَبَّنَا وَاجْعَلْنَا مُسْلِمَيْنِ لَكَ وَمِنْ ذُرِّيَّتِنَا أُمَّةً مُسْلِمَةً لَكَ
Transliteration
Rabbanaa waj'alnaa muslimayni laka wa min dhurriyyatinaa ummatan muslimatan lak
Meaning
Our Lord, make us submissive to You and from our descendants a nation submissive to You.
Reference: Surah Al-Baqarah 2:128
DUA 136

Dua for Steadfastness

Quran • Faith
رَبَّنَا لَا تُزِغْ قُلُوبَنَا بَعْدَ إِذْ هَدَيْتَنَا
Transliteration
Rabbanaa laa tuzigh quloobanaa ba'da idh hadaytanaa
Meaning
Our Lord, do not let our hearts deviate after You have guided us.
Reference: Surah Aal-e-Imran 3:8
DUA 140

Dua for Faithful Believers

Quran • Faith
رَبَّنَا آمَنَّا بِمَا أَنْزَلْتَ وَاتَّبَعْنَا الرَّسُولَ فَاكْتُبْنَا مَعَ الشَّاهِدِينَ
Transliteration
Rabbanaa aamannaa bimaa anzalta wattaba'nar-rasoola faktubnaa ma'ash-shaahideen
Meaning
Our Lord, we have believed in what You revealed and followed the Messenger, so register us among the witnesses.
Reference: Surah Aal-e-Imran 3:53
